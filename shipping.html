Delivery partner

Nita Style works with Wakilni delivery for orders across Lebanon.

Delivery time

Orders are usually prepared and sent within 2-4 working days. Delivery timing may vary depending on the destination, order confirmation, public holidays, or courier availability.

Delivery details

  • Please enter the correct city, street, building, floor, and phone number during checkout.
  • Nita Style may contact you if any delivery detail needs confirmation.
  • Cash on delivery is available for eligible orders.

Return policy

All purchases are final. Nita Style does not accept returns from customers, so please make sure your order details and selected sizes are correct before checkout.

For any urgent delivery question, contact Nita Style through Instagram @thenitastyle or the contact page.
