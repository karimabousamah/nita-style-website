Privacy Policy

Your information

Nita Style collects only the information needed to create your account, prepare your order, deliver your purchase, and contact you about your order when necessary.

How we use it

  • To save your customer account and delivery addresses.
  • To process your cart, checkout details, and order history.
  • To send order confirmation and order status emails.
  • To contact you if delivery details need confirmation.

Privacy commitment

Your details are used only for Nita Style service purposes. We do not sell customer information. Account data can be removed when you delete your account from the account page.

For privacy requests, contact Nita Style through the contact page or Instagram @thenitastyle.
